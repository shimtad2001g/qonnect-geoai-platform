Phase 1: Harvest & Farm Ingestion

Data Ingestion & GeoAI Verification: Upon harvest, the farm boundary is digitized via GPS mobile mapping. The Qonnect pipeline automatically queries Sentinel-2 Level-2A imagery to calculate the NDVI at the exact time of harvest. This ensures that the crop was harvested at peak physiological maturity specific to its species.

Immutable Ledger Entry: A unique Batch ID is generated, and the spatial coordinates, timestamp, and satellite metadata are cryptographically hashed and written to the distributed ledger, ensuring the data cannot be altered retroactively.

×

Phase 2: Quality Processing

IoT Sensor Integration: At the processing center, IoT moisture and temperature sensors directly interface with the Qonnect platform. Moisture content is rigorously verified against the optimal thresholds for the specific crop to prevent spoilage.

Defect Detection: Quality control metrics are logged into the cloud database. The system automatically triggers a verification hash for this processing phase, ensuring that the product moving to export perfectly matches the raw materials harvested in Phase 1.

×

Phase 3: Export & Logistics

Continuous Telemetry: The shipment is equipped with IoT sensors utilizing LoRaWAN/GSM networks. Temperature and humidity are tracked continuously across the transport route. Any anomalies trigger real-time SMS alerts to the logistics manager and the insurer.

Geofencing & Chain of Custody: As the shipment crosses predefined geographic zones (geofences), the status is updated dynamically. Each checkpoint creates a new block in the chain of custody, securely linking the physical location to the digital record.

×

Phase 4: Global Marketplace

Smart Contract Execution: Upon arrival at the international distributor, the buyer scans a QR code linked to the Batch ID. The Qonnect smart contract verifies the entire chain of data—from the satellite NDVI at harvest to the final IoT temperature log.

Parametric Insurance Trigger: If the historical satellite data shows a drought or the IoT sensors detect a severe temperature drop during transport (triggering a multi-peril insurance claim), the smart contract automatically initiates the payout process based on the parametric thresholds set by the insurer.
