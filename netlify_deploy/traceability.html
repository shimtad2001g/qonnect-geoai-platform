Phase 1: Harvest & Farm Ingestion

Data Ingestion & GeoAI Verification: Upon harvest, the farm boundary is digitized via GPS mobile mapping. The Qonnect pipeline automatically queries Sentinel-2 Level-2A imagery to calculate the NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) at the exact time of harvest. This ensures that the crop was harvested at peak physiological maturity (NDVI > 0.75).

Immutable Ledger Entry: A unique Batch ID is generated, and the spatial coordinates, timestamp, and satellite metadata are cryptographically hashed and written to the distributed ledger, ensuring the data cannot be altered retroactively.

×

Phase 2: Quality Processing

IoT Sensor Integration: At the cooperative processing center, IoT moisture sensors directly interface with the Qonnect platform. Moisture content is rigorously verified at 12.0%, which is scientifically optimal for preventing mold and ensuring long-term crop stability.

Defect Detection: Quality control metrics are logged into the cloud database. The system automatically triggers a verification hash for this processing phase, ensuring that the product moving to export perfectly matches the raw materials harvested in Phase 1.

×

Phase 3: Export & Logistics

Continuous Telemetry: The shipment is equipped with IoT sensors utilizing LoRaWAN/GSM networks. Temperature and humidity are tracked continuously across the route to Djibouti Port. Any anomalies (e.g., temperature spikes) trigger real-time SMS alerts to the logistics manager.

Geofencing & Chain of Custody: As the shipment crosses predefined geographic zones (geofences), the status is updated dynamically. Each checkpoint creates a new block in the chain of custody, securely linking the physical location to the digital record.

×

Phase 4: Global Marketplace

Smart Contract Execution: Upon arrival at the international distributor, the buyer scans a QR code linked to the Batch ID. The Qonnect smart contract verifies the entire chain of data—from the satellite NDVI at harvest to the final IoT temperature log.

End-to-End Transparency: Once all conditions are met (quality, logistics, and sustainability metrics), the smart contract executes, authorizing final payment or transfer of ownership. This eliminates fraud, ensures fair compensation for the farmer, and provides the buyer with 100% auditable provenance.
