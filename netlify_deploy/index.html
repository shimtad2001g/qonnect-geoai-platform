Tier 1: Foundation (The Ground)

On-Farm Data Capture & Smallholder Empowerment: Integrates secure digital identification protocols, leveraging biometric fingerprint capture linked directly to smallholder farmers. Utilizes high-precision GPS to map farm parcels and large-scale irrigated fields down to exact boundary polygons.

×

Tier 2: Verification (The Sky)

Satellite Telemetry & GeoAI Intelligence: Continuously ingests high-resolution multi-spectral data from Sentinel-2 (Optical & SAR) for all-weather observation. Machine learning pipelines compute baseline biomass and rigorous tCO2e carbon sequestration models.

×

The Immutable TraceRight Ledger

Core Security & Trust Anchor: Powered by Hyperledger Fabric, this central hub immutably records every transaction. From initial polygon boundary demarcation to final buyer transactions, all data is cryptographically hashed and timestamped.

×

Tier 3: Parametric Crop Insurance

Dynamic GeoAI Risk Engine: A core innovation integrating weather telemetry and satellite data at the farmer parcel level. It automatically triggers rapid, objective insurance payouts upon the occurrence of verified climatic anomalies.

×

Tier 4: Global Market & Monetization

Digital Passports & Carbon Markets: Compiles export-ready verification bundles into a Digital Product Passport with secure QR codes for full chain-of-custody tracking. Converts verified carbon sequestration metrics into standardized digital tokens (tCO2e tokens and NFTs).
