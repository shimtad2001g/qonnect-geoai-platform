Tier 1: Foundation (The Ground)

On-Farm Data Capture & Smallholder Empowerment: Integrates secure digital identification protocols, leveraging biometric fingerprint capture linked directly to smallholder farmers (such as Yohannes Shimeles). Utilizes high-precision GPS to map farm parcels, intercropped fruit trees, and large-scale irrigated barley fields down to exact boundary polygons. Local IoT sensors track real-time farm inputs and agronomic interventions.

×

Tier 2: Verification (The Sky)

Satellite Telemetry & GeoAI Intelligence: Continuously ingests high-resolution multi-spectral data from Sentinel-2 (Optical & SAR) for all-weather observation. Machine learning pipelines compute baseline biomass, canopy height, and rigorous tCO2e carbon sequestration models. Automatically analyzes historical land cover to guarantee EUDR zero-deforestation compliance.

×

The Immutable TraceRight Ledger

Core Security & Trust Anchor: Powered by Hyperledger Fabric, this central hub immutably records every transaction. From initial polygon boundary demarcation to final buyer transactions, all data is cryptographically hashed and timestamped. This eliminates data tampering, ensures complete auditability, and establishes absolute trust between local producers and international regulatory bodies.

×

Tier 3: Parametric Crop Insurance

Dynamic GeoAI Risk Engine: A core innovation integrating weather telemetry and satellite data at the farmer parcel level. It automatically triggers rapid, objective insurance payouts upon the occurrence of verified climatic anomalies (e.g., drought, excessive rainfall), protecting smallholders against crop failure without the need for slow manual claims adjusting.

×

Tier 4: Global Market & Monetization

Digital Passports & Carbon Markets: Compiles export-ready verification bundles into a Digital Product Passport with secure QR codes for full chain-of-custody tracking. Converts verified carbon sequestration metrics into standardized digital tokens (tCO2e tokens and NFTs) via Verra and Gold Standard frameworks, unlocking premium market access and direct carbon monetization for farmers.
